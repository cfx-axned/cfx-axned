### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I've been building in the FiveM ecosystem for 5+ years, from full roleplay servers to premium scripts, web platforms and Discord bots.

- Founder and lead developer of **Vantix Studios**, a FiveM script store for ESX, QBCore and QBox
- Administrator at **CodeM**, one of the largest FiveM creator communities
- Support team member at **FiveGuard** (FiveM anti-cheat)
- Founder of **Imperium Giveaways**, a giveaway platform for FiveM creators
- Freelance developer on **Fiverr**, delivering Discord bots, FiveM servers and custom scripts
- Studying for a BBA at the University of Hertfordshire

### 🛠️ What I Build

- **FiveM scripts** in Lua with React/NUI interfaces, multi-framework (ESX · QBCore · QBox)
- **Web platforms** with Node.js, Express and React, including a custom headless Tebex storefront
- **Tebex store templates** with custom Twig templates and CSS themes
- **Discord bots** with Discord.js, MariaDB and Sequelize, linked to websites through secure internal APIs
- **Server infrastructure** on Linux VPS: Nginx, PM2, SSL, Cloudflare, firewalls
